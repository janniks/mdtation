The page holds still when the file is rewritten, a comment is added or removed, or the window is resized: the cursor line keeps its screen row. Where content went away, empty rows show instead of a jump, and they go as you scroll out of them. A click near the edge no longer scrolls the page. After a rewrite the cursor follows its line by content, also when the line was reworded or moved, and turning flow on keeps it in its paragraph.
