The page holds still when the file is rewritten, a comment is added or removed, or the window is resized: the cursor line keeps its screen row. Where content went away, empty rows show instead of a jump, and they go as you scroll out of them. A click near the edge no longer scrolls the page. After a rewrite the cursor follows its line by content, also when the line was reworded or moved, and turning flow on keeps it in its paragraph. Comments follow their line the same way, across sessions too: a comment no longer vanishes when its heading is renamed, a reply stays with its parent, and a comment whose line was deleted stays next to where it was and prints as `Line N?`.
