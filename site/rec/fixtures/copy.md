# Landing page copy: Tally

## Headline

Your terminal, finally aware of what you are actually working on.

## Subhead

Tally is a lightweight command-line companion that quietly keeps track of every project you touch, so you can stop wondering where you left off and get right back into the flow of whatever it was you were doing before.

## Features

### Picks up where you left off

Every time you open a shell, Tally looks at the directory you are in and shows you a short summary of what happened there last: the branch, the last command that failed, the files you were editing.
No setup, no config file, no daemon.

### Knows which tab is which

When you have a dozen terminals open it is easy to lose track.
Tally names each one after the project and the task, and keeps the name updated as you move around, so you can tell them apart at a glance without reading prompts.

### Stays out of your way

Tally never prints unless you ask.
It adds under two milliseconds to shell startup and stores everything in a single plain-text file you can read, grep, and delete.
